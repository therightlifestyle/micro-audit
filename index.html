Straight answers

The questions a smart customer asks

Honest answers are part of the product.

Is this a sales pitch?

No. It's a working session. You pay for the analysis and the roadmap; implementation is a separate service, and there is zero obligation to buy it. If you take the roadmap and do everything yourself, it still works — that's the point of it.

Do you promise specific results or numbers?

No, and anyone who does should make you careful. We don't use fake metrics or "guaranteed 10x" claims. You get a specific analysis of your business and honest prioritization. If a piece of automation isn't worth doing for your situation, we'll tell you not to do it.

My business is small. Is this worth it?

Small businesses are exactly who this is built for — if you personally handle replies, follow-ups, and daily operations, that's usually where the lost hours hide. If your business already has dedicated teams and mature systems, this audit is less urgent for you, and we'd rather tell you that than sell to you.

What exactly is NOT included?

Implementation. The Micro Audit is analysis and planning only — we show you what to automate and its potential impact; building the systems is our Builder tier, quoted separately if you want it. No surprise invoices, no hidden scope.

What happens after I request a slot?

You message us, we agree on a time, and payment is handled directly and personally when your slot is confirmed — we'll agree on a payment method that works for you. There is no automated checkout, and nothing is charged before we've spoken. Before the call you get a short preparation checklist so the hour is spent on your actual business — not small talk.
